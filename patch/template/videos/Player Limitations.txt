Player Limitations
==================
The Video Player plays what's in this folder, one folder per show or film
(subfolders such as seasons are fine). Things to know before copying videos here:

Size and format
- The screen is 854x480, so 480p (H.264, MP4) is all it can show: it looks
  as sharp as anything bigger, the files are small, and it is the easiest on the
  battery. Use it when you're converting videos yourself.
- 720p H.264 is tested to play at full speed, even at 2x speed. 1080p H.264
  plays too (about 70% of the processor, against about half for 720p), but it
  takes more space and battery and looks no sharper on this screen.
- The chip has no video decoder, so videos are decoded in software. H.265
  (HEVC), 4K and 60 fps videos may stutter or not play. Speeds above 1x are
  smooth only for videos of 720p or less.
- MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, WebM, MPEG and TS files show up. Other files in this
  folder (like this one) are ignored.

Sound and subtitles
- Press the Menu button while a video plays for the video options: subtitles
  (on or off, which track, and their size), the sound track, speed (0.5x to 2x),
  picture (fit, zoom or stretch), play next, a sleep timer, Sound only, and
  start over. They are kept in g36/player.cfg.
- If a file has several sound tracks (languages, commentary), choose one in
  the options. Its subtitle tracks are there too, text or picture.
- Subtitles can also be a file beside the video with the same name:
  Episode.srt, or Episode.en.srt for a language (also .ass, .ssa and .vtt).
  Several are fine; each is offered by name. Files saved as UTF-8, UTF-16 or
  the usual Windows (Western European) encoding show their accents correctly.
  Subtitles in other encodings (Russian, Greek, Japanese, Chinese ...) need
  saving as UTF-8 first; a text editor's "Save as" does it.

Sound only
- "Sound only" in the options turns the screen off and plays on: the picture
  isn't read or decoded at all, so a video you only want to listen to uses
  very little battery. Press any button (or tap Power) to bring the picture
  back where it is. It carries on through the folder (Play next); when the
  folder is finished, the screen comes back on.
- Tapping Power during a video turns the screen off and pauses it. Use Sound
  only when you want the sound to carry on.

Pictures
- Each video's picture is a frame from about a quarter of the way in, made the
  first time the folder is opened (the ones near your selection first). It shows
  big on the right and small beside the video's name in the list, like the games'
  pictures. They're kept in thumbnails/videos/, in the same folders and with the
  same names as the videos. To use your own, replace
  one with your image (JPG or PNG), keeping the name:
  thumbnails/videos/American Dad/Pilot.jpg is the picture for
  videos/American Dad/Pilot.mp4.

Playing and watch progress
- The videos in a folder play one after another, in the order they're listed
  (name them so they sort: "S1 Ep 01", "S1 Ep 02" ...). L and R skip to the
  previous or next one. Left and Right skip 10 seconds, Up and Down a minute.
- Each folder remembers the video you're on and where you stopped in it, not
  every video: opening the folder starts on that video, and any other video
  starts from the beginning. Press X in the Video Player to reset it all.
- Each folder's card shows a bar: "Watching 5/11 videos" (the video you're on,
  out of all of them), then that video and how far into it you are. Folders
  inside the folder, such as seasons, count in order. A folder you haven't
  started just says how many videos it has; one watched to the end says
  "Watched 11/11 videos" until you play it again.
