scripts and so on) are free software under the MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026

CONSOLE LOGOS
-------------
The logos on the console cards (theme/logos/) are from the platform logo set
by Dan Patrick (version 2.1, made for LaunchBox and BigBox, and first shared
on the LaunchBox forum). Most are drawn by him by hand; some are rebuilt from
official artwork. We use the "Normal" size, "Light - Color" versions.

His terms, from the set's own Read Me: the logos can be used elsewhere; he
will always appreciate credit if you use them; he doesn't own the rights to
the logos themselves (they belong to the companies behind each console); and
they are NEVER to be sold in any way.

Changes made here: each logo was shrunk to fit its card (400 x 120 pixels)
and nothing else was changed. Thank you, Dan.


LETTERING
---------
Cards with no logo show their name in Montserrat (theme/fonts/), used under
the SIL Open Font License; its text is in theme/fonts/OFL.txt.


EMULATORS
---------
Games run in RetroArch 1.22.2 and the libretro emulator cores (stored on the
G36-EMU drive): a5200, atari800, fba, fbalpha2012, fbneo, fceumm, fceunext,
gambatte, gearsystem, genesis_plus_gx, gpsp, handy, mame2003, mame2003_plus,
mame2010, mame2016, mednafen_ngp, mednafen_pce_fast, mednafen_pce, mgba,
nekop2, neocd, pcsx_rearmed, prosystem, snes9x2010, snes9x, stella2014,
stella. Each is the work of its own authors, under its own licence; the
arcade cores in particular (FBNeo and the MAME family) are for
non-commercial use only. Thank you to the libretro team and every core author.

BIOS files and game ROMs are not part of this project and are not ours to
give away; they are only on cards for their owner's own games.


GAME PICTURES
-------------
The "Get Artwork" program on the card's drive finds box art for your games on
thumbnails.libretro.com, the libretro thumbnails project (the libretro team and
everyone who made and named those pictures). The pictures belong to their
owners; none are included on this card. They are downloaded on your own
computer, for your own games, only when you run the program. Get Artwork is
our own program (MIT), written in Go; the Go standard library inside it is
BSD-licensed (Copyright The Go Authors). For arcade games
it turns file names such as "2020bb" into titles with a list taken from the
FinalBurn Neo core (see EMULATORS; non-commercial use only).


THE CHATBOT
-----------
A language model, run on the handheld with llama.cpp (Georgi Gerganov and contributors, MIT
licence) and its ggml library: SmolLM2-135M-Instruct, by Hugging Face (the HuggingFaceTB
team: Loubna Ben Allal, Anton Lozhkov, Elie Bakouch and colleagues; Apache 2.0). It is used
as released, in its quantized (smaller) GGUF form. Its paper: "SmolLM2: When Smol Goes Big
-- Data-Centric Training of a Small Language Model".


THE IMAGE GENERATOR
-------------------
  - Supra2-IMG, by SupraLabs (Apache 2.0): the model that draws the pictures.
  - Flan-T5-Base, by Google (Apache 2.0): reads the prompt. Only its encoder
    half is used.
  - TAESD, by Ollin Boer Bohan (madebyollin) (MIT): turns the result into a
    picture, and the sharpening preview.
The three are converted to 8-bit numbers to fit in the handheld's memory
(imagegen/models/); the program that runs them (g36-imagegen) was written for
this project, following Supra2-IMG's own reference code. The pictures it makes
are yours.


VIDEO AND MUSIC
---------------
Playback uses FFmpeg 4.4.4 (the FFmpeg developers; LGPL 2.1 or later, built for
this firmware without any GPL or non-free parts), with the handheld's ALSA sound
library. Picture scaling uses Rockchip's librga, text
drawing uses FreeType, and images are read with stb_image (public domain).

Subtitles can be made on a computer with OpenAI's Whisper, through whisper.cpp
(MIT); the script for that is in the project's tools/ folder, not on the card.


THE MENU'S TEXT
---------------
The menu, chatbot and image generator draw their text in Open Sans Semibold
(Steve Matteson; Apache 2.0), the font the handheld's own firmware came with.


THE HANDHELD ITSELF
-------------------
The handheld (sold as the "G36", built on a Rockchip RK3506B) runs Rockchip's
reference Linux (Buildroot). File transfer over USB uses umtprd; the games
drive is exFAT, made with exfatprogs. Thanks to all of those projects.
