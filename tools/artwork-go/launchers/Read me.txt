GET ARTWORK
===========

Gets a picture for each game on your G36 and puts it where the G36 will find it.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Plug the G36 card into your computer (a card reader or the SD slot).
  2. Double-click:
       Windows:  Get Artwork - Windows.exe
       Mac:      Get Artwork - Mac.command
  3. Answer Y (or just press Enter) when it asks to download.
  4. Put the card back in the G36 and turn it on. Give it a few minutes to turn
     the pictures into thumbnails; they appear in the game lists by themselves.

Your computer needs an internet connection; the G36 doesn't. Run it again whenever
you add games: it only gets pictures for games that don't have one yet, and it
remembers the games it couldn't find (it looks again after 60 days).

WITHOUT TAKING THE CARD OUT
  If the games are on your computer rather than the card, run it, and when it says it
  can't find the card, drag the folder that has your games into the window (the one with
  a folder for each system: gba, snes, ...). It makes a folder called "G36 Artwork"; copy
  what's inside it to thumbnails/to-resize on the G36-GAMES drive (plug the G36 in with
  File Transfer on, or use the card).

If a picture is wrong or missing, put your own in thumbnails/to-resize/<system>/ named
like the game file, e.g. thumbnails/to-resize/gba/Metroid Fusion.png (PNG or JPEG).

The pictures come from the libretro thumbnails project, https://thumbnails.libretro.com .
Command-line options (for the curious): run the program with --help.
